## en-US
**Title (≤30):** Ciclo: period & cycle tracker

**Short description (≤80):** Period, fertile days, symptoms and moods. No account. Your data stays yours.

**Full description:**
Track your period, your cycle and how you feel, with every feature included and nothing to sign up for.

Ciclo learns your own cycle from what you log: when your next period is due, your fertile window and ovulation
day, PMS days, and what tends to come back at the same point of your cycle.

• Today: your cycle day, when your period is due or how late it is, your fertile window
• Calendar: logged and expected periods a year ahead, fertile window, ovulation, PMS days
• Log flow, pain, feelings, energy, sleep, skin, digestion, sex and drive, discharge, tests, medicine,
  exercise, menopause signs and your own tags
• Basal temperature with its curve and cover line; a temperature rise or a positive ovulation test moves the
  prediction
• Weight over time, notes for any day
• Insights: average cycle and period, how much your cycles vary, every cycle as a bar, your patterns
• Modes for trying to get pregnant, pregnancy (week and due date) and perimenopause
• Birth control: pill pack, ring, patch, shot, IUD, implant
• Reminders: period coming, fertile window, pill with a Taken button, ring or patch change, a daily nudge
• Lock with your phone's PIN, pattern or fingerprint
• Share a summary with your partner, never your journal: only what you choose
• A report for your doctor, printed or as a PDF; CSV export
• No account, no ads. Your journal stays on your devices; backups, your other devices and your partner's summary
  travel end-to-end encrypted, so no server can read them
• Open source (Apache-2.0); also on Windows
• English and Spanish

Predictions are estimates from your own logs. They are not a method of birth control.
